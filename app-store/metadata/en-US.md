## Description
Circle the bug. Give your AI the whole story.

MarkuprPlus turns visual feedback into structured context for AI coding agents. Pick a window, region, or display, talk through what is wrong, and mark the screen while you test. When you stop, every finding has the exact frame, timestamp, and narration behind it—ready for Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, or any agent that reads Markdown.

ONE MARK, ONE ACTIONABLE ISSUE

Every completed mark becomes its own finding with a timestamp, annotated screenshot, your words, and capture context. Three circles become three clear issues instead of one ambiguous screen recording.

CAPTURE ONLY WHAT YOU CHOOSE

Exact-window recording stays locked to the selected window as you move, resize, or switch apps. Region and full-display capture are available when you need wider context. MarkuprPlus hides its own picker, recording controls, and drawing canvas from the captured video.

TALK WHILE YOU TEST

Record microphone narration alongside the screen. On-device Whisper can transcribe locally, so your review can stay on your Mac.

CHOOSE HOW AI HELPS

Use deterministic Local Rules with no model, connect to Ollama or LM Studio on your Mac, or bring an Anthropic API key stored in Keychain. MarkuprPlus uses only the provider you select and still writes a local report if analysis fails.

REVIEW BEFORE YOU HAND IT OFF

Reorder findings, edit titles, set category and severity, remove false starts, and preview the exact Markdown your agent will read. Export to Markdown, HTML, JSON, or PDF.

LOCAL FIRST BY DESIGN

No MarkuprPlus account. No developer-operated telemetry. Recordings, audio, screenshots, transcripts, and reports stay locally unless you explicitly choose a cloud provider or share an export.
